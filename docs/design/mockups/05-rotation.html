Section 2 — One rotation cycle

Five minutes on Boston, then the switch. The important bit is what happens at 4:30: the next city's artifact and feed warm up before the cut, so the new map arrives already populated instead of empty-then-filling.

Transition style at the 5:00 cut?

A

Crossfade, ~700ms — recommended

Old network fades out, new fades in. Calm, reads as a slideshow. Cheap on every renderer including a framebuffer.

B

Hard cut

Instant swap. Most "departure board", zero animation cost, but jarring in a dim room.

C

Zoom-out / zoom-in

Pull back from Boston, push into DC, implying a globe. Most striking, but needs both cities' geo anchors and looks wrong when systems differ wildly in extent.
